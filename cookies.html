Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Mr. Elorm Maths and Science Arena uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and login state.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies for essential functions:

  • Session Storage: We use your browser's "Session Storage" to remember your name (e.g., "John") while you move from the Home page to the Quiz page. This is deleted when you close the tab.
  • Third-Party Cookies (Google): Since we use Google Forms for tracking scores and Google Fonts for styling, Google may place cookies on your device to ensure these services work securely.

3. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling them may prevent the website from remembering your name between pages.
