Learning Nuggets 🧠

Did you know? Learning is a superpower.

The Spacing Effect

Spreading out your study sessions over a longer period of time leads to much better long-term retention than cramming.

The Feynman Technique

Explaining a complex concept to someone else is one of the most effective ways to solidify your own understanding.

The Forgetting Curve

Without active review, our brains naturally forget about 50% to 80% of new information within just 24 hours.

Sleep for Storage

Memory consolidation, moving info from short-term to long-term, happens primarily while you sleep.

Metacognition

Learning how you learn—reflecting on your own thinking processes—can significantly boost your academic performance.

Neuroplasticity

Your brain is like a muscle; it physically changes and builds new connections every time you learn something new.
