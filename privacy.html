Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2024

At Mr. Elorm Maths and Science Arena, we prioritize the privacy and safety of our students. This Privacy Policy outlines what data we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to function effectively:

  • Student Name: The name you enter on the homepage is used solely to personalize your experience (e.g., "Welcome, John") and to track quiz completion.
  • Quiz Scores: Your performance data is recorded to help track progress.

We do not request or store sensitive personal information such as phone numbers, home addresses, or credit card details directly on this site.

2. How We Use Data

The information collected is used for educational purposes only:

  • To generate performance reports for students and teachers (if part of an institution).
  • To maintain a leaderboard of top performers.
  • To identify areas where students may need extra help.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power this platform:

  • Google Forms & Sheets: We use Google's secure infrastructure to store student names and scores. By using this site, you acknowledge that your input data is processed via Google.

4. Children's Privacy

This website is designed for educational use by students. We encourage parents and guardians to observe their children's online activity. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information beyond the student's first name/nickname for session tracking.
